# 澄境筆記 0.10.14

- 第二大腦以背景工作執行緒讀取資料與整理關鍵字。每批最多讀取 1,000 張卡片、200 筆待辦、80 張白板，另補入待辦必要的來源卡片；畫面仍最多繪製 200 顆私人神經元。
- 舊卡片不會因日期被刪除。可用「較早內容／較新內容」翻頁，或搜尋未封存內容的完整內文；搜尋結果每類分批載入。神經元使用內容摘錄，原卡片全文保持完整。
- AI 每批整理最多 12 個新增或修改的神經元，搭配相關內容，總共最多 32 個神經元。保存已整理狀態，未變更的同一批不再重送；再次操作可繼續下一批。
- 整理連結完成即保存，不再緊接著自動生成反思。「今日反思」仍可獨立生成。
- 分批整理會保留其他既有 AI 連線與手動連線；套用前重新檢查內容版本。分析期間切換範圍的舊回覆不會套用。
- OpenRouter 會短暫記住已成功的格式相容方式，減少每次先失敗再重試的額外等待。
- 保留 0.10.13 的封存規則、隻言片語卡片化、待辦淡出、標籤樣式和 Android 操作。

在同一台 Mac、10,000 張合成卡片的本機測試中，原本主執行緒重建全圖約 1.71 秒；新背景工作集合約 0.22 秒，且背景讀取期間主畫面仍有回應。這是不同工作量的比較，不代表完整一萬筆分析只需 0.22 秒。AI 參考文字由約 71,000 降到 23,000 字元；實際生成時間仍受模型、排隊與網路影響。

## English

- Second Brain reads data and extracts keywords in a background worker. Each working set contains up to 1,000 cards, 200 tasks, 80 boards, and necessary task-source cards. At most 200 private neurons are rendered at once.
- Notes do not expire by age. Browse older/newer sets or search full text across unarchived content. Search results are paged; neuron excerpts do not alter original notes.
- Each AI batch processes up to 12 new or changed neurons with related context, at most 32 neurons total. Completed content is remembered; unchanged batches are not sent again.
- Links are saved as soon as analysis finishes. Reflection remains a separate action instead of a second automatic generation.
- Existing AI and manual links are preserved. Content versions are rechecked before saving, and replies from an abandoned analysis scope are ignored.
- OpenRouter temporarily remembers successful format compatibility, avoiding repeated failed attempts.
- Preserves 0.10.13 archive, capture-card, task-fading, tag-layout, and Android behavior.

With 10,000 synthetic cards on one Mac, the previous foreground full-graph calculation took about 1.71 seconds; the bounded background working set took about 0.22 seconds while the UI remained responsive. These perform different amounts of work, not a full-dataset speedup claim. AI context fell from roughly 71,000 to 23,000 characters. Provider latency and generation time still vary.
