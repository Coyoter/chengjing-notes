Google 雲端備份現在也適合「寫完就離開」的使用習慣。

- 修改後停筆 30 秒自動備份，閱讀與捲動不影響計時。
- 持續編輯時，依設定的間隔定期備份。
- 完整退出前補存最後變更；失敗或等待過久時可重試、返回應用或仍然退出。
- 尚未成功上傳的變更留待下次開啟補傳；上傳期間新增的內容不會被誤標為完成。
- 保留本地備份、跨裝置衝突保護與前一天緊急救援。

## English

Google cloud backup now supports short writing sessions.

- Automatically backs up 30 seconds after editing stops. Reading and scrolling do not reset the timer.
- Periodically backs up during continuous editing using your selected interval.
- Finishes pending cloud changes before quitting. Failed or slow backups offer Retry, Return, or Quit anyway.
- Pending changes survive restarts, and edits made during an upload remain pending until saved.
- Preserves local backups, cross-device conflict protection, and previous-day emergency recovery.
